## Things with lead time

Anything that depends on another person or on a document you have to make. These get
forgotten until the deadline, so they go here separately.

- [ ] Resume exported to PDF, one page if any org requires it
- [ ] Resume saved under each org's required filename
- [ ] Any link set to public and opened in a private window to confirm
- [ ] Transcript or GPA confirmed if any org asks
- [ ] Anything needing a teammate, a recommender, or a video
