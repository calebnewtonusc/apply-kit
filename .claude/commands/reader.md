---
description: Read the whole packet as the human who actually receives it
---

Load `reader-pov` and follow it. Run on the **whole packet at once**, every file in
`drafts/`, not one club at a time. Most of what this catches only exists across
answers.

Write `review/READER.md`.

Read three times: as the 11pm first-round screener with 200 left, as the specialist
doing diligence who will interview them, and as the future teammate deciding whether
they want to be on a project with this person for fifteen weeks.

Then report what those people notice. Not rule violations. Numbered findings, most
consequential first, each with the quoted answer and an actual recommendation rather
than "consider whether."

Pay attention to the things a per-answer check cannot see:

- Every "why us" answer having the same shape
- A hard subject raised inside a word limit too small to land it
- A reference to something the reader does not know about them
- Tonal whiplash where a joke follows something heavy
- One theme appearing across many answers, reported as texture with a read, not as an
  argument
- Answers noticeably shorter than their limits next to answers at 95 percent

End with the single change that would most improve the packet.

If they push back on a finding, they are usually right. Withdraw it in writing rather
than restating it softer, and keep only the part that survives for a different reason.
