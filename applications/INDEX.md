## Clusters

Groups of clubs whose prompts are close enough that one strong core answer adapts
across all of them. This is what makes eight applications cost the time of three.

For each cluster: which club to write first, since that one becomes the source.

_(filled in by `/targets`)_

## Conflicts

Clubs with overlapping mandatory meetings. You can accept one, and you will be asked
in an interview which you would pick.

_(filled in by `/targets`)_

## The test before submitting anything

Take one answer. Hand a reader only that answer, nothing else. Go down its unasked
list and check whether they could answer each one from it alone.

Three misses is your revision, and the fix is usually a different story rather than
different sentences.
