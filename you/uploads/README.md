# Uploads

Drop things here before running `/intake` and `/voice`. Everything you put here gets
read and mined, and it cuts the interview roughly in half.

## Most useful, in order

**Your resume.** Any format. Every bullet is a story compressed to one line, and "what
happened behind this bullet" is a better interview question than anything the agent
would invent on its own.

**Two or three things you actually wrote.** This is what `/voice` reads so drafts
sound like you. Good samples:

- An old application essay or personal statement
- A cover letter
- A long message or email you wrote to a person, not to a form
- Anything you wrote fast and did not edit much

The last one matters most. Writing you polished for a form shows your careful voice.
Writing you fired off shows your actual one, and the goal is drafts that sound like
the second.

**Past applications, especially ones that worked.** If an answer got you an interview
or an acceptance, say so when you drop it and run `/proven`. Those answers are the most
valuable material in this repo.

**Screenshots or PDFs of the application forms** you are applying to. This is how the
agent gets the real prompts instead of guessing at them.

**A transcript**, if any club asks for GPA or coursework.

## Note

Nothing here leaves your computer. If you plan to make this repo public, uncomment the
`you/` line in `.gitignore` first.
