# Voice

**Template. `/voice` fills this in after reading what you wrote.**

Every section needs quoted examples from your actual writing. A pattern described
without an example is not usable at draft time.

## Sentence rhythm

Average length, and where you go short. Most people have one move: long setup then a
four-word sentence, or the reverse.

> [example]

## How you open

Look at five of your openings and find the pattern. Scene, claim, question, number,
joke. This is the highest-value section in the file.

> [example]

## How you close

Do you land on a concrete detail or reach for a lesson? If you reach for the lesson,
that is the habit most worth breaking.

> [example]

## Vocabulary

Words you use. Words you never use. Register: "kids" or "students," "built" or
"developed," "got" or "obtained."

## Punctuation habits

Comma splices, fragments, parentheticals. The deliberate ones stay. They are the
fingerprint.

## Humor

Do you joke, and how? Dry, self-deprecating, absurd, not at all? An answer written
funny by someone who is not is the most obvious tell in an application.

## Tics to lose

Your own crutches, quoted. Everyone has about three: a repeated transition, a hedge,
one overused adjective.
