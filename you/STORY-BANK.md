## The lead

Your single strongest thing, written as a claim someone could disagree with. Not a
mission statement. Mission statements are not interesting; contestable claims are.

> [the claim]

## Threads

Recurring subjects that can angle toward almost any prompt. For each, one line on what
it is and which kind of club it fits.

## What you believe that people around you do not

The highest-yield thing in intake. Half of all "why do you care about X" prompts are
really asking this.

## What you believed two years ago that you no longer believe

The other half of the prompts.

## Dead lines

Sentences that were true once, or that you have publicly reversed, or that you have
decided not to say again. **Quote them exactly** so no future session reuses them, and
say why each one is dead.
