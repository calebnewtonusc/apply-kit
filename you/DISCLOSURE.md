# Disclosure

**Template. `/intake` fills this in, in your words.**

What you will and will not put in an application. Your call, no one else's. The agent
honors this without relitigating it, every time, in every draft.

## The rule

An application essay is read in a few minutes by a student volunteer scoring a rubric.
That is a real constraint on what can be said well, and it is the reason this file
exists. It is not a judgment about what is worth saying.

The two questions worth asking about anything sensitive:

1. Does this answer have room to land it? A hard subject raised in 75 words gives the
   reader the weight and none of the resolution.
2. Does telling it now cost you the version you get to tell later, from further away?

## Will say

Things you are comfortable having in an application, and how you want them framed.

## Will not say

Things that stay out. No reasons required, though writing the reason helps a future
session understand the line.

## The one-sentence versions

For anything that will come up but that you do not want to explain at length, write
the flat two-sentence version here and **rehearse it out loud until it is boring.**
Unrehearsed, it comes out either apologetic or as five minutes of explanation, and
both are worse than the fact.
