# Outcomes

**`/outcome` fills this in.** What actually happened, per application.

This is the only evidence about what your material does in front of real readers.
Everything else in this repo is general principle. Recording results is what makes the
next cycle start from evidence instead of from enthusiasm.

"Round it ended in" is the most useful column. Cut at the written round is a writing
problem. Cut after an interview is not, and more drafting will not fix it.

## The pattern

What shows up across results that no single application could show. Three applications
is not a pattern. Say so rather than inventing one.
