# Proven answers

**`/proven` fills this in.** Answers from a past cycle that got you an interview or an
acceptance, with the reason they worked.

The most valuable file in the repo and the one most people throw away, because the
prompts changed and the old answers looked useless. The content does not transfer. The
structural moves do.

### [Org], [cycle]. Result: [interview / accepted / waitlisted]

**Why it worked.** The moves, not the content. Where it opens. Whether it names a gap
honestly. How specific the want is. What the last sentence lands on. The beat most
applicants would have skipped. Quote the exact sentence doing the work.

---

## Do not reuse

An answer that worked can still contain something now false: a claim out of date, a
story since reversed, a number since corrected. Passing once does not make it safe to
send again.
