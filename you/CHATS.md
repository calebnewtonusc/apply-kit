# Conversations

**`/chat` fills this in.** Every time you talk to someone at an org you are applying
to: an info session, a coffee chat, a message thread, a panel you asked a question at.

Thirty minutes here produces better material than any amount of searching. It is the
only source of a first-tier specific, the kind nobody who skipped it can write.

## Per conversation

### [Org], [person and role], [date], [how it happened]

**Exact phrases they used about the org.** The most valuable part. An org's own words
about itself, from a member rather than a marketing page, are what make a "why us"
sentence sound like you were there.

>

**What they said the org actually worries about in applicants.**

**Facts.** Project names, the real cycle, the time commitment, whether the published
deadline is the real one.

**Anything they offered, and what you owe them.** Follow-ups and thank-yous go in
`DEADLINES.md` if they have a date on them.
