## Conflicts

Overlapping mandatory meeting times. You can accept exactly one out of each group, and
an interviewer will ask which you would pick, so have the honest answer ready.

## Not actually club applications

Things on the list that turn out to be incubators, programs, or fellowships with
different requirements, cycles, or prerequisites. Each needs its own plan.

## Flags

Anything worth one sentence and then dropping: a club whose stated focus sits oddly
against something you said, a club that rejected you before (which changes what one of
its prompts is really asking), a club whose recruitment could not be confirmed.
